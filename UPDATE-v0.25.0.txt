NEW FEATURES
- Fish feeding, sheltering, travelling and curiosity routines.
- Peaceful Sailfin Gliders and rare Driftbacks, separate from enemies.
- Earned harbour drones with individual activity; moving service equipment.
- Cosmetic surface daylight/weather on a saved twenty-active-minute cycle.
- Wildlife journal: ten optional observations, no reward currency.
- Photo mode: pause/telemetry, frozen visible frame, crop and PNG export.

Shortcuts remain unchanged. J opens the journal on desktop. Use WILDLIFE
inside the existing journal, or its dock Archive / telemetry shortcut.
Photo mode closes back to the paused dive, not directly into action.

Preserved: three campaign slots, upgrades, collision size, credits/crystals,
resource placement and regeneration, ammo prices and respawn stock, recovery
wrecks, contracts, achievements, bosses, repair charge and music.
