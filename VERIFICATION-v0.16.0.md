## Result

**21 automated suites passed:** 18 production-code Chromium suites, one static release-integrity suite and two Node suites with mocked version-checker/worker boundaries. `verification/v0.16.0/results-all.json` records every command/result and the final runtime hashes. All runtime hashes were rechecked against the tested files before packaging. Package verification separately checks complete and update-overlay contents against version.json.

This is NOT a claim of GitHub deployment, successful real service-worker/offline installation, physical Android testing, a human audio review or a complete campaign playthrough.

## New coverage

### Campaign slots

A synthetic save was generated by running the actual archived v0.15 production code, collecting a crystal and creating an active expedition. Its migration into Slot 1 retains currencies, previous upgrade marks, mine ammunition, achievement/stat progress, finite-resource state, survey, wildlife and active ship/cargo state. Both new weapons start unfitted. The original legacy record remains unchanged.

Actual native slot buttons create and name Slots 2 and 3, switch between all three, preserve separate credit/crystal balances and depleted resources, and restore the first expedition paused. All three occupied slots block silent replacement. Delete/cancel controls, deletion of the current slot, choosing another existing slot and pagehide-after-delete are tested. Original legacy data and stale backups cannot resurrect a deliberately deleted slot.

A corrupt primary recovers from a valid backup. Both copies corrupt preserves exact bytes and allows another good slot to be loaded. Simulated quota errors block switching without replacing the last primary. A stale window is prevented from overwriting a newer stored campaign. These storage checks use an in-memory localStorage facade; real-device quota/recovery behavior remains unverified.

### Weapons and damage

All eight new paid upgrade increments were purchased through the real dock UI. Damage, capacities, actual installation reloads, costs and zero grants to paid mine ammunition were checked. The full catalogue now totals 59 steps / 15,173 CR / 464 crystals.

24 projectile impact cases cover both weapons at all four marks against all three predator tiers. Exact damage, single hits, cooldowns and immediate ammunition deductions are asserted. Rock obstruction, torpedo multi-target area damage, ship-safe friendly explosions and detonation of abandoned mines are tested. Empty-magazine guards, no timed/launch refill, actual free dock reload, held keyboard rail and non-repeating torpedo key presses are covered.

Two simultaneous shots in flight were saved from a genuine generated level and restored with identical projectile state, ammunition and cooldowns, paused. New CDP tests exercise simultaneous floating steering, held rail, torpedo and mine at 390x844, 568x320 and 844x390, including release and no stuck firing.

Damage feedback tests cover impact, shield, oxygen and continuous pressure pulses with the intended interval/gap. Actual canvas pixel checks confirm red hull pixels appear in complete darkness and disappear after the pulse without illuminating scenery.

### Interface music

Actual Web Audio buffers and analyser output confirm menu and dock themes produce signal independently from effects and ambience. Both themes are stereo, looping, within the measured peak bound, and have small first/last-sample discontinuities. Repeated scene checks do not create duplicated music sources. Independent volume settings persist. Diving stops music, pause/hidden suspends audio, and interaction is required to start it.

Signal checks are not a human listening review or a guarantee that the mix sounds good on phone speakers. Headphones, physical devices, hearing comfort and battery use remain untested.

## Blocked and untested

A separate real local HTTP/service-worker/offline test was attempted without mocked networking. Chromium rejected navigation with **net::ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ADMINISTRATOR**. See `verification/v0.16.0/live-pwa.json`. Therefore actual worker installation, offline relaunch, hosted cache changes and an installed v0.15 app upgrading to v0.16 are NOT verified. Passing mocked checks does not replace this integration test.

Physical Android/iOS devices, Safari/WebKit/Firefox, device storage quotas, cloud/portable saves, a signed Android build, touch comfort, battery/thermal behavior, human music listening and a full campaign/weapon balance playthrough are untested or outside scope. One generated five-level slot was measured at approximately 324 KB of JSON; that sample is not a worst-case storage guarantee. Local storage failures are detected, not made impossible.

## Reproduction

`python tests/run_v0160.py --jobs 3` runs current suites. The slot migration suite uses the original archive when available and the retained synthetic fixture otherwise. `python tests/v0160_live_pwa_test.py` is the separate live integration attempt. Rebuild `version.json` with `python tests/build_release.py` after runtime changes. Historical verification documents describe historical builds, not this release.
