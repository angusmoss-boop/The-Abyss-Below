# The Abyss Below v0.17.0: Salvage & Supply

Build `0.17.0-salvage-supply-1`. Update base: v0.16.0 / Arsenal & Archives.

## The loop

Explore for scrap, bank it for credits, purchase ammunition and ship improvements, then return to the deeper objectives. Salvage is renewable income; crystals remain finite campaign progression material. Currency balances are separate. This release changes the economy and collection behaviour without moving terrain, crystals, wrecks or saved encounters.

## Slow salvage recovery

Original scrap deposits recover at their existing generated locations. A collected deposit becomes eligible after **300 seconds (five minutes) of active dive time**. A visited level then restores at most **one eligible deposit every 45 seconds**. A recovered deposit normally yields two scrap, worth 36 credits when returned safely. Restored stock cannot exceed the map's original number of scrap sites.

Timers advance for every already-generated level in the currently loaded campaign while the submarine is outside the protected harbour on an active, unpaused dive. They do not advance in menus, at the dock, inside the safe harbour, while another campaign is loaded, or while the game is closed. This is simulation time, not the device's date/time. No offline windfall or system-clock exploit.

A current-level deposit must be outside the camera view plus a 100-world-unit margin and beyond magnet range plus another 100 units before it can return. A mature site in view simply waits. There is no accumulated bulk-spawn allowance: after one recovery the next waits another 45 seconds. Other visited levels have independent, saved recovery queues.

Crystals, secured recorders, recovery pods and predator drops are not regenerated by this system. Predators and emergency supply caches retain their existing genuine-dock-return refresh rule. Predator kills still drop 2 / 4 / 8 scrap. A kill drop can be collected only once; it does not become another regeneration site.

Docking and level switching do not instantly restore depleted scrap or reset timers. Three campaign slots keep separate clocks, due times, world state and stocks. The survey map retains known sites; the expedition selector shows current **SCRAP AVAILABLE**, rather than implying a permanently diminishing pool.

## Automatic salvage, deliberate crystal collection

Scrap, including predator salvage, is collected automatically when reachable. A standard ship has a **6 m** close-recovery radius. Salvage Magnet Mk I / II / III / IV extends it to **10 / 14 / 18 / 22 m**.

At close range collection is free and still works with an empty battery. At extended range the fitted magnet requires a visible deposit (ambient light, flashlight or sonar echo), clear line of sight through actual collision geometry and enough battery for its normal 1.2 / 1.0 / 0.8 / 0.6 power cost. Nothing is pulled through rocks. At most one deposit is collected per 0.25 seconds, with the nearest eligible scrap chosen first. A brief tether trace and pickup sound provide feedback without repeated notification spam.

**Crystals and recorders still require E / USE.** Supply caches and recovery pods also remain manual. Crystals retain manual magnet-assisted reach; recorders and caches require a close approach. USE ignores ordinary scrap so it does not repeatedly take priority over a crystal or recorder. The mobile steering and action controls are unchanged.

Clean Sweep and Full Survey now explicitly count collecting each original scrap/crystal site at least once. Renewing a scrap site does not undo the exploration record, and recollecting it cannot count as a new site. Existing achievement timestamps remain intact. Automatic extended-range pickups count toward Magnetic Attraction.

## Paid dock ammunition

The AMMUNITION tab contains separate cards for all three types:

| Type | Price per round | Mk I / II / III / IV capacity |
|---|---:|---|
| Pulse Railgun | 6 CR | 12 / 18 / 24 / 32 |
| Shock Torpedoes | 24 CR | 3 / 4 / 6 / 8 |
| Proximity Mines | 18 CR | 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 |

Buy one round or fill available capacity. When the wallet cannot fund a full fill, the bulk button offers only the affordable quantity and displays the actual charge. Full/unfitted/unaffordable states are disabled. Purchases require the actual physical dock, the ammunition tab and a fitted weapon; they do not spend crystals.

**No weapon receives free rounds from docking, installing or upgrading gear, dying, relaunching, loading a save or switching campaigns.** Increasing capacity adds empty space and preserves rounds already owned. The existing paid mine-stock rules remain, with its price raised from 12 to 18 credits.

Dock hull repair, oxygen, power, shield and boost restoration remain free. Field repair still consumes two scrap for 30 hull; it is not a credit transaction. This keeps recovery viable when the wallet and weapon magazines are empty. Salvage exploration does not require ammunition.

Weapons' damage, fire rates, safety locks, projectiles and existing magazine capacities are unchanged. Rail fire costs 18 credits to kill a 100-HP drifter with three Mk I hits, yielding 36 credits in recovered salvage. Two Mk I torpedoes cost 48 credits; firing both at a single such target is uneconomical, while their area damage can reward hitting a group. These are arithmetic examples, not a guarantee every engagement is profitable.

## Existing saves

The same three campaign-slot keys and campaign schema 3 remain in use. Each field gains a versioned `salvage` record containing elapsed dive time, the next pacing deadline, due times by existing scrap ID and a first-harvest ledger. Validation accepts only original scrap IDs in that ledger and recovery queue. Crystal IDs and predator-drop IDs cannot enter it.

Old depleted scrap begins a five-minute recovery when that slot is first loaded by this release; nothing immediately refills on migration. Previously visited original sites are marked harvested for achievements. Uncollected sites and all finite crystal depletion remain unchanged. Active-dive cargo, ammunition, projectiles, enemies, wildlife and survey data migrate without granting new stock.

Existing gun and mine rounds are retained without a retrospective bill. Already purchased upgrades are retained without a price adjustment. Inactive slot files are not rewritten until selected. Saves remain local to the browser/origin, not cloud saves; clearing site data can destroy them.

## Installation and verification

Save or bank the current expedition as a precaution and close duplicate game windows. Apply the root-level contents of the update ZIP over **v0.16.0**, keeping the same site address. Do not clear storage or start a new campaign to update. The complete ZIP is the standalone source alternative.

Publish the changed runtime assets and version.json together. Reopen online and verify **v0.17.0 / Salvage & Supply** on the main menu. The version checker still requires consistent published hashes, loaded build markers and a controlling worker before claiming sync. A local/unverified preview is not a hosted release.

Run `python tests/build_release.py` after any runtime change. Current tests are `python tests/run_v0170.py --jobs 3`; the real HTTP/worker test is separate at `python tests/v0170_live_pwa_test.py`. See VERIFICATION-v0.17.0.md for current results and boundaries. Historical guides describe older economies and do not override this one.
