Build: **0.12.0-fivefold-descent-1**. Figures below describe a fresh campaign unless explicitly marked as migration behaviour.

Counts refer to resource units, not map icons. Each fixed scrap deposit and crystal cluster contains two units. Supply caches and predator drops are separate from the fixed-resource totals and survey achievements.

Every level guarantees at least 16 scrap units (288 credits) and 12 crystal units inside standard-hull depth. Its saved resource layout is generated once when first selected. A cleared deposit remains cleared through all normal visits and reloads. Only a confirmed New Game starts new resource maps.

Banking all recorders unlocks the next destination. Banking is essential: a recorder carried aboard is not yet secured. The EXPEDITIONS hub tab displays unlock status, recorder progress, floor depth and remaining resource stocks. Returning to an earlier unlocked level is free and never consumes ammunition or rerolls deposits.

### Increasing danger

Predator base health and damage are multiplied by 1.00 / 1.04 / 1.08 / 1.15 / 1.23 across the five destinations. Predator density also increases. Base Drifter/Stalker/Reaper HP remains 100 / 200 / 400; base bite damage is 13 / 27 / 42. Speeds retain the previous release's values, so a Reaper still exactly matches Mk IV normal thrust rather than outrunning every non-boosting ship by an arbitrary multiplier.

Terrain has denser obstacles deeper into the campaign. All five seabeds are real collision boundaries; reaching the bottom does not wrap the ship or silently clamp the HUD while allowing the capsule through. Sonar can reveal the seabed outline. The last hull mark is rated slightly beyond the final floor so the bottom is reachable without pressure damage.

Mk I reaches the first seabed; Mk II reaches the second; Mk IV reaches the third; Mk VI reaches the fourth; Mk VII reaches the last. Levels do not require every optional system to unlock.

The shop protects progression by examining each intermediate hull step needed to reach the deepest recorder in the highest unlocked destination. An optional purchase cannot spend crystals that would make a hull step unaffordable even after collecting the still-available crystals reachable before that step. Deep crystals are not counted ahead of their pressure gate. For an unvisited, partly reachable map, only its guaranteed shallow starter stock is counted until the layout has been generated. The message explains which hull mark needs priority.

A survey counts collected fixed deposits, not whether every recovered unit has subsequently been banked. Predator salvage and recovery pods do not create additional fixed deposits. Banking achievements count delivered units, not current unspent wallet balances. Successful sonar pulses count only when the action actually fires. Safe-return tracking resets on docking and requires a real departure, sufficient depth and cargo delivery.

Stored best depth, already-secured recorders and already-maxed systems can satisfy derived achievements on migration. Historical kills, shield absorption and other unrecorded old counters cannot be reconstructed, so those begin with the new tracker. Confirmed New Game resets achievements as well as levels and currencies. There is no platform-account or Google Play Games achievement integration.

## Recovery and persistence

Picking up permanent resources writes both the depleted deposit and unbanked cargo into one campaign snapshot. If the dive ends by defeat, deliberate quit or reload, that cargo becomes one saved recovery pod. Claim it and dock to bank the resources. The same pod is removed when collected, so repeated reloads do not mint currency or restore harvested deposits.

A pod is clamped to the fitted hull's safe depth minus a margin and placed on a clear navigation route when required. It is discoverable with sonar and the normal nearby objective indicator. Recorders that were not banked return to their wrecks. Used ammunition and repair materials remain spent. A new recovery replaces carried resources, not the entire ship state.

The ship does not resume its exact location, health, oxygen, planted mines or AI simulation after reload. Saves depend on browser storage; site-data deletion, changing browser/origin or unavailable storage are not recoverable without an external backup. A previous atomic snapshot is retained as a fallback for malformed primary JSON. This is not a cloud-save or comprehensive corruption-repair guarantee.

## v0.11 campaign migration

- Bank cargo on v0.11 before installing this update. The new recovery journal cannot retroactively protect an unsaved old dive.
- Credits, XP, best depth, upgrades and owned mine rounds carry forward with no retrospective crystal charge.
- The old single trench becomes Level 5. Its seed, original terrain generation, deposit IDs/coordinates/depletion, old population IDs/damage and secured recorders are retained. Its old recorder depths remain 75 / 260 / 470 / 725 / 1,100 / 1,500 m, avoiding relocated or duplicated mission progress.
- Access starts at Level 1. Clear the four new levels in order to revisit the preserved old trench. Previously secured final-level recorders still count and are not deleted while access is locked.
- On first selecting the imported final level, crystal deposits are added once to bring its original generated crystal-stock total up to 160. For a standard old layout this adds 112 crystals in 56 new clusters. It does not respawn any old collected deposit or refund sold crystals.
- The old map contained more scrap than a fresh final-level layout; imported resource totals may therefore exceed the fresh-campaign table. Its starting crystal wallet is zero because the previous game sold crystals as credits, and those credited funds remain retained.
- The new canonical key is `the-abyss-below-campaign-v3` (schema 3). Old keys are read-only migration sources until an explicit New Game clears them. Migration tests use a synthetic save created by the real archived v0.11 runtime, not anyone's personal save.

## Scope and evidence

Progression gates, arithmetic, collection/docking paths, purchases, migration and achievement triggers have automated checks. The tests use synthetic equipment and repositioning to isolate cases; they are not a human-paced campaign completion. Balance may need further tuning after actual play.
